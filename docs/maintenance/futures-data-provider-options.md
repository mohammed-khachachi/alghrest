## Correcting the blanket non-display claim

“Any algorithm or data younger than 24 hours always incurs an additional
non-display exchange fee” is not a safe universal rule. The **January 2025 CME
non-display FAQ** distinguishes real-time data within ten minutes, delayed data
between ten minutes and eight hours, and historical data beyond eight hours.
It includes research/risk analytics as possible non-display uses and states that
historical-only non-display use must be licensed but carried no fees/reporting
at that document's date. It refers readers to current agreements for controlling
terms. [Official CME FAQ](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/distributor/files/cme-group-data-licensing-policy-guidelines-and-non-display-licensing-faq.pdf).

Databento separately describes its historical/live boundary around 24 hours and
states historical access has no monthly license fees. Its portal explains that
API access does not automatically constitute non-display use at every venue;
personal/commercial classification and supported uses vary. These vendor terms
do not replace a user's applicable exchange/provider agreement.
[Quickstart](https://databento.com/docs/getting-started/live),
[licensing portal documentation](https://databento.com/docs/portal).

The current **June 2026 CME fee list** ties non-display fees to applications
accessing real-time or delayed information, with qualifying user categories.
Do not transfer benchmark-specific 24-hour definitions to MES/MNQ futures.
[Fee list](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/files/june-2026-market-data-fee-list.pdf).
ALGHREST remains advisory-only, but that fact alone neither proves an exemption
nor fixes the fee category. Obtain confirmation for private analytics, storage,
replay, number of devices and prospective prop-evaluation use. No firm-level
tariff or non-professional attestation was assigned to this user in this review.

## Data suitability for ALGHREST

**Engineering recommendation, not a purchase instruction:** compare a bounded
Databento historical-only pilot with a FirstRateData individual-contract quote
before considering a live subscription. Independent historical procurement can
be assessed separately from an Earn2Trade platform/API connection. DataMine is
another direct historical route; IBKR/Tradovate introduce broker-account/API
dependencies which are not necessary merely to inspect historical files.

Require exact expiry identities and original prices for exposure/P&L. Continuous
series can support research only with an explicit causal roll rule and mapping;
adjustment does not remove roll costs or convert the series into one tradable
contract. FirstRateData documents a mapping file, Eastern-time bar starts and
omitted no-trade minutes, and excludes contract specifications/calendars. Its
single-ticker product is downloaded via the customer page; API archives are
bundle-only. [FAQ](https://firstratedata.com/about/FAQ).

Minute OHLCV can support an initial bar-quality study but does not reveal the order
of within-bar events or executable spreads. For realistic intraday evaluation
paths, determine whether trade/quote data and synchronized bid/ask marks are
needed before buying. A bid/ask requirement is not automatically a requirement
for a full MBO archive. Existing chronological/holdout/cost restrictions apply.
No strategy tuning, real holdout access or probability estimate occurred here.

Before acquiring a pilot, record:

1. Provider permission for local storage, private analysis/replay and retention;
   user classification and any required additional agreements.
2. Dated MES/MNQ contracts, range, schema, timezone/DST and session conventions;
   raw versus adjusted prices, roll mapping and correction/version identity.
3. A quote for the actual pilot scope, with spend limit, delivery/update costs and
   historical/live distinction. Displayed list fees alone are insufficient.
4. Dataset/code hashes and the existing offline intake result; missing sessions,
   sequence/quote evidence or costs must remain explicit.

The current [offline preflight](futures-data-access.md#offline-intake-implemented)
accepts the internal snapshot format, not every provider's raw CSV/DBN unchanged.
Any future conversion must reuse that interface, preserve original files and
be tested on permitted or labelled synthetic samples. Provider integration stays
gated until the grant and requested scope are established.
